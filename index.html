INSCRIPTIONS

Notes from the night

What is my goal? +
Survive longer than your last run. Push kills, score, and time. XP and gold you gather upgrade future runs. The dungeon does not end. You do.
Is it free to play? +
Yes. Connect a wallet and enter. No purchase to crawl the night. Play-to-earn sits on top of that — it is not a ticket in.
What is permadeath? +
Every death ends the current run. No continues. Orbs and growth carry forward. The run itself is gone. Die trying. Return stronger.
What resets after a run? +
In-session level and the elemental skills you rolled this crawl go back to zero. Every run is a new draw. Growth you banked stays.
How do orbs work? +
Monsters drop orbs. Run over them. XP levels you in the session. Health, armor, and stamina keep you in the horde longer.
What do pets do? +
Gen 1 pets follow you on their own. No extra buttons. They add utility, pressure, or sustain. Three roles. They change how a run lasts.
Can I play on mobile? +
Yes. Browser on PC and mobile. Touch controls adapt. The horde does not care what you hold.
Wanna play the game? +
Yes. Open insomnus.xyz. Connect a wallet, then enter access code 8BWIBF. Crawl the night.
What is this list? +
A chance to mint a Gen 1 pet before public. Applying is free. It does not guarantee a mint. Seal the slip, then earn points.
How do I climb the board? +
Follow, Discord, like, repost, comment, and share on X each pay once. Your referral link is +80 every time someone seals through it.
