Notes from the night

What is the petlist? +
A list of wallets that stand a chance to mint a Gen 1 pet before public. Applying costs nothing. It does not guarantee that you will get a pet.
When is the mint? +
When the date is announced you will hear it on @insomnusxyz. This page is the application, not the mint.
What does it cost? +
Applying is free. Mint price is announced with the mint. One pet per wallet on the list. Gas is on you.
Do I need to hold anything? +
No. No token, no NFT, no anything. Just the three lines on the slip, a follow, and a quote or reply on the pinned post.
