Notes from the night

What is the petlist? +
A list of wallets that stand a chance to mint before public. Applying costs nothing. It does not guarantee a mint.
When is the mint? +
When the date is announced you will hear it on @insomnusxyz. This page is the application, not the mint.
What does it cost? +
Applying is free. Mint price is announced with the mint. One spot per wallet on the list. Gas is on you.
Do I need to hold anything? +
No. No token, no NFT, no anything. Just the three lines on the slip, a follow, and a quote or reply on the pinned post.
How do referrals work? +
Connect Google first. Your referral link still uses your X handle: ?ref=yourname. Anyone who seals a slip through that link is +80 for you. Follow Insomnus, Follow KokoApe, and Discord are +100 each. Like is +5, repost is +10, comment is +15, sealing the slip is +50.
How do the X tasks work? +
Tap a card. It jumps to X or Discord. Follow, Discord, like, repost, and comment each score once. Share is the only one that can score again — +80 per referral.
Why connect Google? +
So each person has one login we can rank. Google Sign-In is free. We only read your Google name, email, and avatar. You still add your X handle on the slip for the quote and referrals.
